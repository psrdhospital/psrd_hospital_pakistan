Why Rehabilitation Centers Are Important

Rehabilitation centers play a vital role in restoring physical, mental, and emotional health. They help patients regain independence and improve quality of life.

Benefits include:

  • Professional medical supervision
  • Structured recovery programs
  • Psychological support
  • Physical therapy and mobility training
  • Addiction recovery programs

Types of Rehabilitation Services in Pakistan

1. Physical Rehabilitation

Focused on patients recovering from injuries, surgeries, strokes, or disabilities. It includes physiotherapy and occupational therapy.

2. Addiction Rehabilitation

Helps individuals recover from drug or alcohol dependency through counseling, detox, and behavioral therapy.

3. Neurological Rehabilitation

Designed for patients with brain or spinal cord injuries and neurological disorders.

4. Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Specialized care for bone fractures, joint replacements, and musculoskeletal conditions.
